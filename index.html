OUR HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

Where Parisian Soul Meets
Karachi’s Cultural Haven.

Founded in December 2015 by Head Chef Fahim Jaffer, Côte Rôtie was born out of a profound passion for rustic, honest French bistro dining and time-honored European culinary craftsmanship.

Nestled inside the serene grounds of the Alliance Française de Karachi in Clifton, the restaurant is a quiet oasis away from the city's ceaseless momentum. Surrounded by sun-dappled courtyard foliage and classical French architecture, guests gather over handcrafted sourdough loaves baked fresh every morning, delicate French tartines, and meticulously finished mains.

From our slow-simmered sauces to our renowned dark chocolate tart, every plate is an exploration of restraint, quality ingredients, and welcoming hospitality.
